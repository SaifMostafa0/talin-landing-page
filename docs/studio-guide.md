# Talin website: editing guide

Use Talin Studio to publish Insights articles and open roles on talindata.com. Everything else on the website is fixed copy; contact the web team to change it.

Open the Studio at **https://talin.sanity.studio** and sign in with the account you were invited with.

## Publish an article

1. Choose **Insights articles**, then the **+** (new) button.
2. Fill in every field:
   - **Title**
   - **Web address**: click **Generate** to create it from the title. Don't change it after the article is live, because shared links would break.
   - **One-line summary**: shown on the article card and under the title.
   - **Topic**: one of the four topics.
   - **Publication date**: articles are listed newest first.
   - **Article text**: use **Section heading** for the main headings. Every image needs a short description for screen readers.
3. Click **Publish**. The article appears on the website within about a minute: on the Insights page, and under "Latest insights" on the home page.

Drafts are never shown on the website, only published versions.

## Fix or remove an article

- **Fix:** edit the article and click **Publish** again.
- **Remove:** open the **⋯** menu and choose **Unpublish**. Choose **Delete** only if it should be gone for good.
- **Undo a mistake:** open the **⋯** menu, choose **Review changes**, and restore an earlier version.

## Add or close an open role

1. Choose **Open roles**, then **+**.
2. Enter the **Role title** and the **Link to the role's full details**, a public `https://` page where candidates can read the role and apply.
3. Optionally set **Position in the list** (lower numbers appear first).
4. Click **Publish**.

When a vacancy closes, **Unpublish** or **Delete** the role. When no roles are published, the Careers page points visitors to the CID Consulting careers page.

## If something looks wrong

Any entry with a missing or invalid field is left off the website rather than shown broken. If an entry doesn't appear, check every field is filled in and published. The web team can also hide or replace any entry without using the Studio.
